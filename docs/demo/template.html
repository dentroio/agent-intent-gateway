Limits

    
    
      
  • Paraphrase. Data an LLM rewrites in new words carries none of the original values. Value-level rules either let it through (residual: allow) or ask a human. That is the one bypass in the hand-tuned run.
    • 
      
  • Prompt-injection influence. Whether injected text steered a decision is not visible in values, so integrity rules stay session-level and stricter than strictly needed.
    • 
      
  • Lab identity. Tokens come from a mock issuer shaped after the AegisFlux design. Production identity waits on the AegisFlux token issuer and steering gateway.
    • 
      
  • Prototype. Python, one simulated enterprise, scripted attacks. Competitor descriptions come from public and vendor sources and were not tested here.
